CLO Records: terms and privacy

CLO Records is a personal, read-only app. One family uses it to keep a copy of one patient's own health records on that family's own computer, with the patient's permission.

  • It only reads records. It never changes anything at a health system.
  • It is not offered to the public, sold, or paid for by advertising. No one's data is sold or used for marketing.
  • Records go from the health system to a folder on the family's own computer. That folder is backed up by the family's own iCloud Drive account.
  • The family uses an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) on that computer to read and organize the records. When it does, the parts it reads are sent to Anthropic to be processed.
  • A few people the family chooses, such as relatives, can view a private summary page from their own devices over an encrypted connection.
  • Apart from that, the developer runs no server and sends records to no one else.
  • The patient (or her authorized MyChart proxy) signs in on the health system's own page. The app never sees or stores a password.
  • The family keeps its copy of the records until it deletes them. Access can be ended at any time in MyChart, under linked apps, with "Stop sharing".

This site holds only the app's public signing keys (non-production, production), which contain no personal or health information.
